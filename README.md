# A Zero-Shot Foundation Model Ensemble for Regional Probabilistic Solar Power Forecasting

Yang Long, Shahid Nawaz Khan, Bo Liu, Qihui Yang, Hongyu Wu, Yichen Liu

Submitted to the 2027 IEEE PES General Meeting.

## Status

This repository will host the materials of the paper. They are being prepared and will be released upon publication.

## Planned contents

- Preregistration documents of the three evaluation stages, with their SHA-256 hashes and OpenTimestamps proofs.
- Predictions of the prospective test (forecast origins Oct. 17 to Nov. 4, 2026), with the manifests and OpenTimestamps proofs submitted on each issue day. Origins Oct. 17 to 20 come from a prespecified as-of replay; the other 15 origins were produced in real time.
- Source code of the forecasting pipeline (TimesFM-3 and Chronos-2 ensemble with HRRR covariates and LLM-derived textual features) and of the evaluation.
- LLM-derived scores extracted from the NWS Area Forecast Discussions.
